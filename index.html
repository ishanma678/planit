How it works

Simple enough for spontaneous plans. Structured enough for real events.

PlanIt keeps decisions out of scattered texts. Every invite has people, place, time, comments, status, and notifications.

01

Create the plan

Add a title, optional description, date, time, place, and the people you want to invite.

02

Send it once

Saved contacts make repeat invites easier, and share links help people who have not joined yet.

03

Track who is in

Accepted, declined, pending, cancelled, and expired states stay organized by invite.

04

Keep everyone updated

Edits, cancellations, comments, replies, and reminders notify the right people.

Features

Built around the life of an invite.

From the first idea to the final reminder, PlanIt keeps the whole plan readable.

RSVP tracking

See accepted, declined, and pending guests by name, not just by number.

Push notifications

Invite updates can reach users even when the PWA is closed on supported devices.

Reminder alerts

Guests can be reminded before a plan starts so fewer people miss the time.

Invite comments

Plan threads keep questions and replies attached to the invite instead of buried in chat.

Edit after sending

Creators can update time, place, description, and add more people later.

Saved contacts

People you invite or accept can be saved for faster planning next time.

Install PlanIt

Works like an app from your home screen.

PlanIt is a progressive web app. Open it in Safari or Chrome, add it to your home screen, and use it like a normal mobile app.

Open PlanIt app
iPhoneOpen in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
AndroidOpen in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app.
NotificationsAllow notifications after login to receive invite updates and reminders.

FAQ

Questions people search before trying an invite app.

What is PlanIt?

PlanIt is a mobile invite planning app for creating plans, sending event invites, tracking RSVPs, reminders, comments, and guest activity.

Does PlanIt work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. PlanIt is built as a PWA, so it can run on iPhone, Android, and supported mobile browsers.

Can I see who accepted or declined?

Yes. Plan creators can open a plan and see accepted, declined, and pending people by name.

Can guests comment on an invite?

Yes. Invited people can leave threaded comments so plan details stay connected to the event.
